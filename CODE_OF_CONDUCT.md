# Code of Conduct

Pixpal is a small, friendly project. Everyone is welcome here, whatever their background or experience.

- Be kind and patient. Assume good faith.
- Disagree with ideas, not people. No harassment, insults or discrimination.
- Keep issues and pull requests on topic.

Maintainers may edit, close or remove contributions that break these rules, and may block people who keep breaking them. To report a problem, open a private security advisory on the repository or contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile.
